# 0004: Reversible Navigation Sequence with Dual Hotkeys and Sub-Step Ticks

Live presentation decks must feel natural whether controlled by keyboard arrows, spacebar, or physical presentation clickers. We decided to bind forward navigation to `ArrowRight`, `Space`, and `PageDown`, and backward navigation to `ArrowLeft` and `PageUp`. Navigation steps first exhaust all intra-slide build steps reversibly before crossing slide boundaries. Segmented step ticks in the slide footer give the presenter at-a-glance visibility into remaining clicks per slide.
